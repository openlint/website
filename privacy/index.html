Privacy

openlint.org has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies of its own. This page says what happens to your data when you visit it, and when you use the OpenLint tools.

Last updated 29 October 2026.

Who we are

OpenLint is a community open-source project, an API Commons effort, with Open Source Europe as its fiscal host. Questions about this page go to info@openlint.org.

This website

  • Hosting. openlint.org, docs.openlint.org and spec.openlint.org are static pages served by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and request details when you load a page, as any web host does, and handles them under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't receive or keep those logs.
  • No analytics, no tracking. The site loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and sets no cookies.
  • Embedded videos. Meeting pages embed the recording from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube receives data from your browser when the player loads, and more if you play the video, under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Email. If you write to info@openlint.org, we use your message and address only to reply and to run the project, and don't share them.

Meetings and community spaces

Office hours are recorded, and the recording, a transcript and a summary are published on this site and on YouTube. If you join, what you say and your name may appear in them. Ask at info@openlint.org and we'll correct or remove your name or contributions from a transcript. Issues, discussions and pull requests live on GitHub and are public, under GitHub's terms.

The OpenLint tools

The OpenLint CLI and packages run on your machine and send no telemetry. They don't send your API descriptions or rulesets anywhere. The only network requests they make are the ones your ruleset or documents ask for, such as an extends URL or a remote $ref, and installing the packages from npm, which npm's privacy policy covers.

Your rights

If you're in the EU or UK, you have rights under the GDPR to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Because we keep almost none, the usual request is about a meeting transcript or an email. Write to info@openlint.org.

Changes

Changes to this page go through a public pull request on openlint/website, so its history is the record of what changed and when.
